# IKMA_Controls_Gamepad.md

Inscryption Kaycee's Mod Access, control reference.
Current as of build v0.7.415.

The controller version of IKMA_Controls.md, GENERATED from it (Session 34) with the same key-to-button rules the mod uses when it speaks (PadWords.cs), so the two cannot disagree. Button names are Xbox; PlayStation, Steam Deck and Switch use their own names for the same positions (docs/GAMEPAD_MAP.md). Hold LB for the information buttons, hold RB for the action buttons. Rumble follows the controller; it is off while you are using the keyboard.

## Keys that work almost everywhere

- View: help for the screen you are on. It lists that screen's keys.
- X: repeats where you are, or describes what is in front of you.
- Menu: opens or closes the pause menu. On a menu screen it goes back.
- LB plus RB plus View: saves a log file to your desktop for a bug report. Works at any point.
- L3: looks up the rulebook entry for the abilities you heard most recently.
- Y: opens the rulebook, wherever the rulebook can be reached.
- LB plus View: the help list. The same help as View, one line at a time: D-pad Up and Down move, B, Menu or LB plus View closes it.
- LB plus Menu: Mod Settings. Events lists every kind of announcement IKMA makes on its own; each can be switched off, or kept only in the review history. D-pad Up and Down move, A opens or switches, B goes back.
- R3: silences speech, the line being read and everything waiting behind it.
- LB plus RB plus D-pad Up and Down: step through the review history, newer and older.

## Conversations

While a character is talking, the game waits for you.

- X: advance to the next line.
- D-pad, A and Y: say "Conversation in progress, press X to proceed." The rulebook does not open during a conversation.
- View and Menu still work.

## The map

- D-pad up and down, or D-pad left and right: browse the paths ahead.
- A: travel to the path you are on.
- X: where you are, and the choices ahead.
- LB plus B: read the paths ahead.
- Right stick up: display your deck.
- Right stick down: stand up from the table.
- RB plus X: hear your current items.
- Y: open the rulebook.
- View: map help.

## Standing in the cabin

After right stick down on the map.

- D-pad up: step forward. D-pad down: step back.
- D-pad left and right: step to the side.
- LB plus A, or right stick left: turn 90 degrees counterclockwise.
- RB plus A, or right stick right: turn 90 degrees clockwise.
- X: describe what is directly in front of you.
- RB plus D-pad up, right and down: examine what is in front of you.
- Y: open the rulebook.
- B: step back from something you are focused on. From standing, returns you to the table and opens the map.
- View: standing help, or focus help while you are focused on something.

## Battle: your turn

### The draw phase

At the start of your turn, before anything else:

- RB plus A: draw from your deck.
- RB plus B: draw from the Squirrel deck.
- X: repeat the draw prompt.

If a deck is empty, the draw phase tells you so and names the other key.

### Your hand

- D-pad left and right: browse your hand.
- LT: jump to the next card you can play.
- A: play the card you are on.

### Reading the board

These also work while placing a card, choosing sacrifices and choosing an item target.

- LB plus D-pad up: read the enemy queue.
- LB plus D-pad right: read the enemy board.
- LB plus D-pad down: read your board.
- LB plus D-pad left: read your hand.
- LB plus B: read the full board and queue.
- LB plus A: read the scales, your bones and other game info.
- X: says you are in an encounter, and to press View for help.

### Items

- RB plus X: open the item menu. Press RB plus X again to cycle through your items.
- LB plus X: read all three item slots, empty ones included.
- RB plus D-pad up, RB plus D-pad right, RB plus D-pad down: use the item in that slot directly.

In the item menu:

- D-pad left and right: browse the items you are carrying.
- A: use the item you are on.
- B: back to your hand.
- LT: leave the item menu.
- X: where you are. View: item menu help.

### Ending your turn

- RT: ring the bell and end your turn.

### Surrender

When Leshy offers the olive branch, the mod says so after his line and again after each draw.

- RB plus Y: accept his surrender.

## Placing a card

After A on a card that needs a slot.

- D-pad left and right: move between slots.
- RB plus D-pad up, right, down, left: jump straight to that slot.
- LT: jump to the next slot the card can go in.
- A: place the card.
- B: cancel and return the card to your hand, where the game allows it.
- View: placing help.

## Choosing sacrifices

- D-pad left and right: move across your board.
- RB plus D-pad up, right, down, left: jump straight to that slot.
- LT: jump to the next card you can sacrifice.
- A: sacrifice the card you are on.
- B: cancel, where the game allows it. A sacrifice cannot be cancelled once one card has been taken.
- View: sacrifice help.

## Choosing an item target

- D-pad: move between the cards the item can affect.
- A: choose the target.
- B: says the item cannot be cancelled. Once used, an item must be given a target.
- View: target help.

## Choosing a card from your deck

For effects like Hoarder and Magpie's Glass.

- D-pad: browse your deck.
- A: take the card you are on.
- X: repeat your position.
- LB plus D-pad right, LB plus B and LB plus A: read the board.
- View or LT: help.

## Trading pelts in battle

The Trader's battle, where pelts in your hand buy cards from his side.

- D-pad: browse what can be traded for.
- A: trade a pelt for the card you are on.
- X: repeat your position. View: help.

## Node screens

Campfire, Woodcarver, Bone Lord's altar, sacrifice stone, Mycologists, item pickups, Trapper, Trader and the rest.

- D-pad: browse the options.
- A: choose the option you are on.
- X: repeat your position.
- Y: open the rulebook.
- Right stick up: display your deck, where the screen allows it.
- RB plus X: read the item being offered, on an item pickup.
- View: help for that screen.

At the Woodcarver:

- LB plus A: open your backpack of carvings.
- B: close the backpack.

At the Trapper:

- LB plus A: count your teeth.
- B: leave the Trapper.

## Card choice

Picking a new card after a battle or at a choice node.

- D-pad: browse the cards.
- A: take the card you are on.
- X: repeat the current option.
- Y: open the rulebook.
- Right stick up: display your deck.
- View: help. When you have a Lucky Clover, help says how to use it.

## Your deck

Opened with right stick up.

- D-pad: browse the cards.
- X: repeat the card you are on.
- LB plus A: how many teeth you have.
- RB plus X: cycle through your items, one per press.
- Y: open the rulebook.
- B, RB plus A or right stick up: close your deck.
- View: help.

## The rulebook

- D-pad left and right, or D-pad up and down: turn pages.
- X: repeat the current page.
- B or Y: close the book.
- View: help.

## Menus

The Kaycee's Mod menus: starting a run, starter decks, challenges, and the rest.

- D-pad: browse the options.
- A: choose the option you are on.
- B or Menu: go back.
- X: repeat the current option.
- View: help.

## The pause menu

- D-pad: browse the options.
- A: choose.
- X: your position and the run information.
- B or Menu: close the menu.
- View: help.

In options:

- RB: next page right. LB: next page left. The pages wrap round. Page 1 is game settings, page 2 display, page 3 audio.
- D-pad: browse the settings.
- A: select a setting to adjust. Then the D-pad changes it, and B stops adjusting.
- B or Menu: leave options.

## Title and start-up screens

- D-pad: browse. A: choose. X: repeat. View: help.
- Menu on the title screen: says the video logs are not available yet.

## Run end screen

- X: repeat the screen.
- A: take the option on it.
- B: back.
- View: help.

## Credits

- B or Menu: leave the credits.

## Screens IKMA cannot read yet

- X: describe what is in front of you.
- View: what still works here.
