# IKMA_Controls.md

Inscryption Kaycee's Mod Access, control reference.
Current as of build v0.7.415.

Rebuilt from the mod's source (HotkeyManager.cs and the help lines the mod speaks), not from the previous version of this document. Enter always means either Enter key, including the one on the number pad. Shift means either Shift key.

## Keys that work almost everywhere

- H: help for the screen you are on. It lists that screen's keys.
- Space: repeats where you are, or describes what is in front of you.
- Escape: opens or closes the pause menu. On a menu screen it goes back.
- Shift+L: saves a log file to your desktop for a bug report. Works at any point.
- Shift+R: looks up the rulebook entry for the abilities you heard most recently.
- R: opens the rulebook, wherever the rulebook can be reached.
- F1: the help list. The same help as H, one line at a time: Up and Down move, Home and End jump, Backspace, Escape or F1 closes it.
- Control plus M: Mod Settings. Events lists every kind of announcement IKMA makes on its own; each can be switched off, or kept only in the review history. Up and Down move, Enter opens or switches, Backspace or Escape goes back.
- Tap Control: silences speech, the line being read and everything waiting behind it.
- Control plus Up and Control plus Down: step through the review history, newer and older.
- Y: the review history as a list.

## Conversations

While a character is talking, the game waits for you.

- Space: advance to the next line.
- Arrows, Enter and R: say "Conversation in progress, press Space to proceed." The rulebook does not open during a conversation (Session 34).
- H and Escape still work.

## The map

- Up and Down arrows, or Left and Right arrows: browse the paths ahead.
- Enter: travel to the path you are on.
- Space: where you are, and the choices ahead.
- B: read the paths ahead.
- Shift+Up: display your deck.
- Shift+Down: stand up from the table.
- I: hear your current items.
- R: open the rulebook.
- H: map help.

## Standing in the cabin

After Shift+Down on the map.

- Up arrow: step forward. Down arrow: step back.
- Left and Right arrows: step to the side.
- A, or Shift+Left: turn 90 degrees counterclockwise.
- D, or Shift+Right: turn 90 degrees clockwise.
- Space: describe what is directly in front of you.
- 1, 2 and 3: examine what is in front of you.
- R: open the rulebook.
- Backspace: step back from something you are focused on. From standing, returns you to the table and opens the map.
- H: standing help, or focus help while you are focused on something.

## Battle: your turn

### The draw phase

At the start of your turn, before anything else:

- D: draw from your deck.
- S: draw from the Squirrel deck.
- Space: repeat the draw prompt.

If a deck is empty, the draw phase tells you so and names the other key.

### Your hand

- Left and Right arrows: browse your hand.
- Tab: jump to the next card you can play.
- Enter: play the card you are on.

### Reading the board

These also work while placing a card, choosing sacrifices and choosing an item target.

- C: read your hand.
- G: read the enemy board.
- Shift+G: read your board.
- U: read the enemy queue.
- B: read the full board and queue.
- A: read the scales, your bones and other game info.
- Space: says you are in an encounter, and to press H for help.

### Items

- I: open the item menu. Press I again to cycle through your items.
- Shift+I: read all three item slots, empty ones included.
- Shift+1, Shift+2, Shift+3: use the item in that slot directly.

In the item menu:

- Left and Right arrows: browse the items you are carrying.
- Enter: use the item you are on.
- Backspace: back to your hand.
- Tab: leave the item menu.
- Space: where you are. H: item menu help.

### Ending your turn

- E: ring the bell and end your turn.

### Surrender

When Leshy offers the olive branch, the mod says so after his line and again after each draw.

- Shift+E: accept his surrender.

## Placing a card

After Enter on a card that needs a slot.

- Left and Right arrows: move between slots.
- 1, 2, 3, 4: jump straight to that slot.
- Tab: jump to the next slot the card can go in.
- Enter: place the card.
- Backspace: cancel and return the card to your hand, where the game allows it.
- H: placing help.

## Choosing sacrifices

- Left and Right arrows: move across your board.
- 1, 2, 3, 4: jump straight to that slot.
- Tab: jump to the next card you can sacrifice.
- Enter: sacrifice the card you are on.
- Backspace: cancel, where the game allows it. A sacrifice cannot be cancelled once one card has been taken.
- H: sacrifice help.

## Choosing an item target

- Arrows: move between the cards the item can affect.
- Enter: choose the target.
- Backspace: says the item cannot be cancelled. Once used, an item must be given a target.
- H: target help.

## Choosing a card from your deck

For effects like Hoarder and Magpie's Glass.

- Arrows: browse your deck.
- Enter: take the card you are on.
- Space: repeat your position.
- G, B and A: read the board.
- H or Tab: help.

## Trading pelts in battle

The Trader's battle, where pelts in your hand buy cards from his side.

- Arrows: browse what can be traded for.
- Enter: trade a pelt for the card you are on.
- Space: repeat your position. H: help.

## Node screens

Campfire, Woodcarver, Bone Lord's altar, sacrifice stone, Mycologists, item pickups, Trapper, Trader and the rest.

- Arrows: browse the options.
- Enter: choose the option you are on.
- Space: repeat your position.
- R: open the rulebook.
- Shift+Up: display your deck, where the screen allows it.
- I: read the item being offered, on an item pickup.
- H: help for that screen.

At the Woodcarver:

- A: open your backpack of carvings.
- Backspace: close the backpack.

At the Trapper:

- A: count your teeth.
- Backspace: leave the Trapper.

## Card choice

Picking a new card after a battle or at a choice node.

- Arrows: browse the cards.
- Enter: take the card you are on.
- Space: repeat the current option.
- R: open the rulebook.
- Shift+Up: display your deck.
- H: help. When you have a Lucky Clover, help says how to use it.

## Your deck

Opened with Shift+Up.

- Arrows: browse the cards.
- Space: repeat the card you are on.
- A: how many teeth you have.
- I: cycle through your items, one per press.
- R: open the rulebook.
- Backspace, D or Shift+Up: close your deck.
- H: help.

## The rulebook

- Left and Right arrows, or Up and Down arrows: turn pages.
- Space: repeat the current page.
- Backspace or R: close the book.
- H: help.

## Menus

The Kaycee's Mod menus: starting a run, starter decks, challenges, and the rest.

- Arrows: browse the options.
- Enter: choose the option you are on.
- Backspace or Escape: go back.
- Space: repeat the current option.
- H: help.

## The pause menu

- Arrows: browse the options.
- Enter: choose.
- Space: your position and the run information.
- Backspace or Escape: close the menu.
- H: help.

In options:

- 1, 2, 3: switch pages. Page 1 is game settings, page 2 display, page 3 audio.
- Arrows: browse the settings.
- Enter: select a setting to adjust. Then arrows change it, and Backspace stops adjusting.
- Backspace or Escape: leave options.

## Title and start-up screens

- Arrows: browse. Enter: choose. Space: repeat. H: help.
- Escape on the title screen: says the video logs are not available yet.

## Run end screen

- Space: repeat the screen.
- Enter: take the option on it.
- Backspace: back.
- H: help.

## Credits

- Backspace or Escape: leave the credits.

## Screens IKMA cannot read yet

- Space: describe what is in front of you.
- H: what still works here.
